Privacy Policy — Daily Pet Videos

Effective date: 8 November 2026

What this app does

Daily Pet Videos is a small automation app that uploads short animal and pet videos (dogs, cats, parrots) to its own YouTube channel, “daily pet videos” (youtube.com/@daily_pet_videoss). It uses the YouTube Data API v3 with only the youtube.upload scope, and only to upload videos to that one channel.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal data from anyone else. The app accesses only the channel owner's own YouTube channel, for the sole purpose of uploading that channel's videos. No viewer data, no comments, and no other account data are accessed.

Data sharing

No data is shared with any third party. Videos uploaded through the app are published by the channel owner on their own YouTube channel, where YouTube's own privacy policy applies to viewers.

Data retention

The app does not keep a database of user data. Authorisation tokens used to access the owner's own channel are kept private and are never published or shared.
